# Martyrdom of St Colluthus — normalized Sahidic edition (word-divided, section-numbered)

Pierpont Morgan Codex **M591, ff. 88v–92v** (facsimile plates 178–186). This is the public-domain **Hyvernat 1922** photographic facsimile with word division applied, nomina sacra
expanded, and section numbers added at the sense-breaks. 

## Conventions
- **Word division** applied to the diplomatic *scriptio continua* per standard Sahidic morphology
  (articles, prepositions, verbal prefixes, etc.). Letters are **not** altered or "corrected"; the
  scribe's actual spellings are kept (e.g. ⲇⲁⲍⲓⲙⲟⲛⲓⲁⲛⲟⲥ for expected Maximianus). Words split over a
  manuscript line break are joined.
- **Nomina sacra expanded in (round brackets):** ⲡⲉⲭ̄ⲥ̄ → ⲡⲉⲭ(ⲣⲓⲥⲧⲟ)ⲥ; ⲭ̄ⲥ̄ → ⲭ(ⲣⲓⲥⲧⲟ)ⲥ; ⲓ̄ⲥ̄ →
  ⲓ(ⲏⲥⲟⲩ)ⲥ; ⲡⲛ̄ⲁ̄ / ⲡⲉⲡⲛ̄ⲁ̄ → ⲡⲉⲡⲛ(ⲉⲩⲙ)ⲁ. Ordinary supralinear strokes (over syllabic ⲛ̄ ⲣ̄ ⲙ̄ and
  the article ⲡ̄) are **not** bracketed — they simply resolve into normal word division (ⲡ̄ϩⲏⲅⲉⲙⲱⲛ →
  ⲡϩⲏⲅⲉⲙⲱⲛ, ⲛ̄ⲣ̄ⲣⲱⲟⲩ → ⲛⲣⲣⲱⲟⲩ). ⲛⲟⲩⲧⲉ / ⲡⲛⲟⲩⲧⲉ is left written in full (uncontracted). Diaeresis on
  ⲓ/ⲩ is kept.
- **Uncertainty preserved:** `⟦?⟧` = doubtful glyph (carried from the diplomatic file); `[…±N]` =
  lost ink, N letters estimated; `⟦word-div?⟧` = a word-division I judged genuinely ambiguous.
- **Punctuation** (· : and paragraph points) kept as in the diplomatic text.
- **§ = sense-break** (speaker change, usually marked by ekthesis / large initial and by the tags
  **ⲡⲉϫⲉ ⲡϩⲏⲅⲉⲙⲱⲛ** "the governor said" / **ⲡⲉϫⲉ ⲡϩⲁⲅⲓⲟⲥ ⲕⲟⲗⲟⲑⲟⲥ** "the holy Colluthus said").
- **Locator tags** (e.g. `[p03 a.1–8]`) map each section back to `coptic-diplomatic.md`; read order
  is p01→p09, col. A then col. B per page.
- **Foliation caveat:** our `f.NN` = the Morgan Library online viewer's `f.(NN+1)`.
- On the **ⲁⲅⲓⲟⲥ / ϩⲁⲅⲓⲟⲥ** question: the scribe writes both ⲡ̄ⲁⲅⲓⲟⲥ and ⲡ̄ϩⲁⲅⲓⲟⲥ for "the holy
  (one)". Per the "do not alter letters" rule each instance keeps its own spelling (article overline
  resolved: ⲡ̄ⲁⲅⲓⲟⲥ → ⲡⲁⲅⲓⲟⲥ, ⲡ̄ϩⲁⲅⲓⲟⲥ → ⲡϩⲁⲅⲓⲟⲥ); the variation is noted in **Method**.

## Method

**Scope.** This file is a mechanical-plus-morphological transform of the PD **Hyvernat 1922** facsimile of M591. The p02 effaced block is an **explicit lacuna**, not reconstructed.

**Sections.** 63 sections (§1–§63) were created at the sense-breaks: the rubric title (§1), the
narrative frames, and each speaker turn — keyed to the tags **ⲡⲉϫⲉ ⲡ̄ϩⲏⲅⲉⲙⲱⲛ** / **ⲡⲉϫⲉ ⲡ̄ⲁⲅⲓⲟⲥ
(ⲡ̄ϩⲁⲅⲓⲟⲥ / ⲡ̄ⲡⲉⲧⲟⲩⲁⲁⲃ / ⲡ̄ⲇⲓⲕⲁⲓⲟⲥ) ⲕⲟⲗⲟⲑⲟⲥ**, plus the orators (ⲛⲉⲣⲏⲧⲱⲣ; **ⲃⲏⲥⲁⲙⲱⲛ**,
**ⲃⲓⲏⲣⲏⲙⲓⲁⲥ**) and the court officers (**ⲛ̄ⲁⲧⲧⲁⲝⲓⲥ**, οἱ τῆς τάξεως) — and at the noted ekthesis/large
initials (e.g. p04 a.9, a.12, a.15, a.18, a.23, a.27; p05 a.19; p09 a.19).

**Nomina sacra** were expanded in round brackets exactly per the conventions key
(ⲡⲉⲭ(ⲣⲓⲥⲧⲟ)ⲥ, ⲓ(ⲏⲥⲟⲩ)ⲥ, ⲡⲉⲡⲛ(ⲉⲩⲙ)ⲁ); ⲛⲟⲩⲧⲉ/ⲡⲛⲟⲩⲧⲉ left in full; ordinary supralinear strokes
(incl. the article ⲡ̄) resolved silently into word division, not bracketed.

**PD tools consulted:**
- **Coptic Dictionary Online (KELLIA, coptic-dictionary.org)** and **Crum** — used *as reference
  knowledge* for standard Sahidic morphology and word-division (articles ⲡ/ⲧ/ⲛ, genitive/attributive
  ⲛ̄-, prepositions ϩⲛ̄/ⲉ/ⲙ̄/ⲛ̄, verbal prefixes ⲉ-/ⲛ̄ⲧⲁⲩ-/ϣⲁⲣⲉ-/ⲛ̄ⲅ̄-, negatives ⲛ̄…ⲁⲛ, causative
  ⲉⲧⲣⲉ-) and for Greek-loan identifications flagged in-line (βῆμα, ἀπόφασις, ἀναγκαῖον, σύνοδος,
  συμβουλεύειν, ἐγκαλεῖν, κατέχειν, τολμᾶν, εὐχαριστεῖν, φιλανθρωπία, ἀπολογία, βάσανος, τέως,
  οὐκέτι, ὄντως, and the uncertain πραιτώριον / πείθειν / κλέος).
- **Onomastics** spot-check: the recurring names were normalized to their established Sahidic forms
  and left **exactly as the scribe writes them** — **ⲕⲟⲗⲟⲑⲟⲥ** (Colluthus), **ⲁⲣⲓⲁⲛⲟⲥ** (Arianus the
  ἡγεμών), **ⲁⲛⲧⲓⲛⲟⲟⲩ** (Antinoöpolis), **ⲇⲓⲟⲕⲗⲏⲧⲓⲁⲛⲟⲥ** (Diocletian), and the scribe's
  **ⲇⲁⲍⲓⲙⲟⲛⲓⲁⲛⲟⲥ** (for Maximianus, *not* corrected). These match the standard forms of the Colluthus
  dossier; **Giorgi 1793** (the PD Coptic+Latin *miracle* recension in this folder) was available as
  the onomastic cross-reference, but the name-forms here are the well-attested standard ones and did
  not require emendation from it, so no text was drawn from Giorgi.
- **No copyrighted-edition text was used.** Reymond & Barns and Schenke were **not** consulted; where
  the diplomatic reading cannot be settled without them — above all p02's lower two-thirds — the crux
  is **left flagged**, not filled. The only place any free oracle informed a reading is p09's ending,
  inherited from Stage 3 (Cult of Saints **E00664**, used there only to disambiguate our own reading
  of the plate — the date **24 Pashons**, the sentence, ⲟⲩⲉⲟⲟⲩ / ⲁϥⲣ̄ϣⲁ ⲙⲛ̄); those spots are flagged
  in §55/§59/§62 above.

**On ⲁⲅⲓⲟⲥ vs ϩⲁⲅⲓⲟⲥ.** The scribe alternates ⲡ̄ⲁⲅⲓⲟⲥ and ⲡ̄ϩⲁⲅⲓⲟⲥ for "the holy (one)". Honoring
the "do not alter the letters" rule, each instance keeps its own spelling with only the article
overline resolved (ⲡ̄ⲁⲅⲓⲟⲥ → ⲡⲁⲅⲓⲟⲥ; ⲡ̄ϩⲁⲅⲓⲟⲥ → ⲡϩⲁⲅⲓⲟⲥ), rather than being leveled to a single
form. The conventions-key example (ⲡ̄ⲁⲅⲓⲟⲥ → ⲡϩⲁⲅⲓⲟⲥ) is noted; the variation is preserved so a
Coptologist can see it.

**Honesty note.** This is a strong base text for a translator and a Coptologist to verify, **not** a
final critical edition. Every diplomatic uncertainty survives; the p02 effaced block is an explicit
lacuna, not filled; new word-division judgment calls are flagged in Cruxes §B.
