# Origen, *Homilies on Genesis* (*In Genesim*) — Latin source (Rufinus's version)

Sixteen homilies of Origen on the book of Genesis, surviving not in the largely lost
Greek but in the Latin translation made by **Rufinus of Aquileia** (early 5th c.). This
folder holds the Latin base text used for the English translation in the main repository.

## Base text
- **Edition:** W. A. Baehrens (ed.), *Origenes Werke* VI — *Homilien zum Hexateuch in
  Rufins Übersetzung*, 1. Teil, **GCS 29** (Leipzig, 1920), *In Genesim homiliae I–XVI*.
- **Digital source:** the public-domain page scans of *Origenes Werke* Bd. 6 (1920) on
  archive.org; the running text was reconstructed from their OCR.
- **Public domain.** The 1920 edition is long out of copyright; a fresh English
  translation from this text is clean to publish.

## Collation
Every homily was collated **page by page against the printed scans** of GCS 29 (the
critical edition's main text — not Migne/PG 12, and not the apparatus variants). Multiple
cleaning-and-validation passes caught and corrected OCR residue in the running text
(dropped words, wrong-but-valid word swaps, base-vs-variant readings, mis-scoped
scripture marking) against Baehrens's printed line.

## What was stripped
Removed from the running text: the critical apparatus, running heads, marginal
Lommatzsch/Delarue references, and stichometry/page numbers. Kept: all of Rufinus's
running Latin.

## Editorial marks
- `« … »` wrap the scriptural quotations and echoes that the printed page marks with
  `» … «` (Baehrens's German-oriented guillemets, flipped to French orientation here).
- `‘ … ’` (curly single quotes) mark Origen's name-glosses and coined well-names
  (e.g. ‘iniquitas’, ‘inimicitia’, ‘amplitudo’), kept distinct from scripture citation.
- `[ … ]` are Baehrens's own editorial brackets (e.g. `qui[a]`, `[eius]`), and
  `* * * *` marks a lacuna he prints — both preserved exactly as in the edition.
- Baehrens's spellings are kept (Iesus, Istrahel, Moyses, Isaac, Iacob, Iohannes,
  coelum/coeli, foenum, Matthaeus, Job, Judam …); printed *J* is left as the edition prints.

## Structure markers
- `== HOMILIA N ==` headings (N = 1–16), with the edition's descriptive *capitulum*
  (`De …`) kept on the line below the heading.
- `[§N]` markers follow **Baehrens's printed section numbering** within each homily.

## Files
- `homily01_latin.txt` … `homily16_latin.txt` — the sixteen homilies, in order.
