# Jerome, Commentary on Galatians — Vers. 17, 18 (Gal 2:16b-18). Book I: lemma and commentary on Gal 2:16b-18.
# Jerome's own Latin (written at Bethlehem c. 386 for Paula and Eustochium, drawing on Origen's lost commentary); survives complete.
# Source text: Migne, Patrologia Latina 26 (Paris 1845), col. 344 (reprint of Vallarsi, Opera t. 7, Venice 1769).
# Scripture is quoted in Jerome's own wording (his Old-Latin-based text of Paul, not harmonized to the Vulgate).
============================================================
(Vers. 17, 18.)
*Propter quod ex operibus Legis non justificabitur omnis caro. Quod si quærentes justificari in Christo, inventi sumus et ipsi peccatores: numquid Christus peccati minister est? Absit. Si enim quæ destruxi, hæc iterum reædifico, prævaricatorem me constituo.*
Non justificabitur [Al. justificatur] ex operibus Legis caro illa, de qua scribitur: »Omnis caro fenum; et omnis gloria ejus, quasi flos agri« (Isai. xl, 6). Justificatur autem ex fide Jesu Christi caro illa, de qua in resurrectionis dicitur sacramento: »Omnis caro videbit salutare Dei« (Luc. iii, 6). Sed et juxta humiliorem intellectum, justificabatur quondam ex Lege non omnis caro, sed tantum hi homines qui in Palæstina erant. Nunc autem ex fide Jesu Christi justificatur omnis caro, dum Ecclesia ejus in toto orbe fundatur.
